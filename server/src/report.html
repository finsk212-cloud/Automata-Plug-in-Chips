Gear check

Testing with late-game gear in an early world makes numbers look huge: weapon damage and Plugin DPS go up with gear, while a plugin's own damage grows only with the world stage. This check leaves out weapons that are too strong for the stage, using three signs: the weapon's rarity compared with the stage (when the mod sent it), damage per second and per hit both far above other weapons at the same stage, or already as high as weapons several stages ahead. It is a best guess, so look at the list and use Skip or Keep on any row. Your choices are saved in this browser only.

Rarity table: from which stage each weapon rarity belongs (editable placeholders)

Newer versions of the mod send each weapon's rarity. A weapon used more than "slack" stages before the stage its rarity belongs to is left out, even when no other player has uploaded anything to compare with. The defaults are my guesses, not facts: rarity is only a rough sign of progression (for example the Uzi is lime, a late rarity, yet it can drop in early Hardmode, and Duke Fishron's yellow weapons can be had much earlier than other yellow ones). How to read this page
Boost: the extra damage a plugin adds on top of the weapon. +25% means the weapon does 100 and the plugin adds 25 more. The verdict is based on this, because it stays fair across weak and strong weapons.
Verdict: Too strong and Strong are Boost over the lines of the plugin's rarity. Low means under 30% of the Strong line (change this under Balance targets). Normal is in between. Plugins that deal no damage, or have no weapon to compare, are not judged.
Plugin DPS: plugin damage per second of fighting. Weapon DPS: the weapon's own damage per second. vs peers: Plugin DPS divided by the median plugin at the same stage and weapon class. These are context only: they rise with the player's gear, so late-game gear in an early world makes them skyrocket (see Gear check).
Gear check: finds weapons that are far too strong for their stage and leaves them out. Boost is the safest number, but it is not immune: a plugin with flat damage looks weak next to a huge weapon, and a plugin that hits other enemies can look strong because the weapon's recorded damage is capped at the enemy's remaining life.
Data: good is 10+ uploads and 2+ minutes, some meets your minimums, few does not. Only good rows reach the lists at the top.
Consistency: how much a plugin's Boost changes from one upload to the next. Steady is reliable. Swingy means a few heavy uploads may be pulling the average. It needs 3+ uploads from the new version on, so older rows show a dash.
Rarity ladder: the median Boost of good rows for each rarity. It should climb from Common to Legendary.
